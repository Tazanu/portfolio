Flagship In active development

StudyHub

A peer-to-peer e-learning platform for university students. Students form study groups and chat in real time, book sessions with tutors, ask and answer questions, and buy or share lecture notes.

The hard parts were the money and the trust: payments run through mobile money rather than cards, so the backend has its own transaction reconciler and payment-health checks, plus content-quality and uploader-trust scoring to keep the notes marketplace usable.

02 — About

Who's building it

I'm a software developer who likes the whole span of a product — deciding what the data should look like, building the API around it, and then making the interface that sits on top feel obvious to use.

Most of my recent work has gone into StudyHub, and it's taught me more than any tutorial did: what happens when real payments fail halfway, why you need read state on a message table, and how much of "a feature" is actually the error handling around it.

I care about fast load times, accessible interfaces, and code the next person can read. Lately I've been exploring where small, well-scoped AI and ML features genuinely help — the dropout-risk model and the assistant on this page both came out of that.
